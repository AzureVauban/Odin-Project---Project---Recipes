Ingredients

  • 0.75 lb Cooked Turkey Meat – shredded or cubed for texture
  • 2 stalks of Celery – finely chopped
  • 2 Green Onions – thinly sliced
  • 0.50 lb Red Bell Pepper – diced into small pieces
  • 3 Tbsp Mayo – use full-fat for creaminess
  • 2 Tbsp Dijon-style Mustard – for a tangy kick
  • 1 Tbsp Apple Cider Vinegar – to balance flavors
  • 1 Tbsp White Sugar – to enhance the overall taste
  • 0.25 Tbsp Salt – adjust according to your preference

Note: This recipe is designed to yield approximately 24 servings – ideal for large gatherings or meal prep throughout the week.

Instructions

  1. Place the turkey, celery, green onions, and red bell pepper in a food processor and pulse until the ingredients are evenly chopped (do not over-process).
  2. Transfer the chopped ingredients to a large mixing bowl. Add the mayo, Dijon mustard, apple cider vinegar, white sugar, and salt, stirring thoroughly to blend the flavors.
  3. Cover the bowl with plastic wrap and chill in the refrigerator for at least 8 hours to allow the ingredients to meld and develop a richer taste.

Storing Instructions

Store the turkey salad in an airtight container in the refrigerator. When properly prepared, it should remain fresh for 3 to 5 days. Recipe originally adapted from this amazing gobble gobbler recipe.
